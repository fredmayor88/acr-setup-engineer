# Suspension Setup, Adjustments, and Testing - Team O'Neil
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EDmHpxgMxFU

- usually 50% stiffer springs vs the road version

their order:
- spring rates
- ride height
- ARBs
- wheel angles in order: caster, camber, toe 
- dampers
- tire pressure
- brake bias 


Although their order is dictated by practicality: adjust what's difficult to adjust first (in the garage) and later finer adjustments can be done e.g. on stage

the base setting: the factory one
on rough road: up ride height and low the spring rate: high and soft. ARBs softer. high speed compression down. slow compression up. more tyre pressure on rough road. 
slippery road - softer springs
grippy road - stiffer springs 
basically for suspensions and ARBs - like for rough vs smooth road 

slippery road: low pressure
grippy road: high pressure 

brake bias: the better the grip the more towards front 

    
if the car understeers: go bottom to the top!!!
- first front tyre pressure lower. and rear higher 
- dampers: low speed rebound: front softer, rear higher  
- for understeer: toe out
