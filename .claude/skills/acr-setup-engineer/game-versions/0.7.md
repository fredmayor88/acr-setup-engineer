# ACR 0.7 — tuning notes for this game version

What is true about the game in this version and not necessarily in others. Workflows read this
file as the guideline layer right after the base principles: it overrides
`setup-tuning-principles.md` where it names a rule, and the user's own guidelines (global,
surface, per-car) and the driving intent still win over it. Quote it as "0.7 tyre notes" when a
value comes from here.

## What changed in 0.7

Read from the game files. The catalog and the bundled default setups already carry these; this
list is so the reasoning can name them.

- **New cars:** Lancia Delta S4 (Group B) and Lancia Delta S4 Evo2 Italtecnica (Group S), both
  four-wheel drive with a mid-mounted engine and Center Differential Ratio as the only gearing
  setting.
- **Rally2 engines are detuned.** Hyundai i20 Rally2 and Skoda Fabia RS Rally2: peak power down
  from about 320 hp at 7250 rpm to about 290 hp at 5000 rpm, less torque above 3000 rpm. VW Polo
  GTI R5: a little less torque above 6000 rpm. Fiat 131 Abarth: more mid-range torque.
- **Damper ranges moved on most cars**, mostly to lower minimums (softer settings are now legal).
  Subaru Impreza springs are softer across the range; the Alpine A110's front anti-roll bar goes
  much stiffer; centre LSD preload goes higher on the Xsara WRC, 206 WRC and Impreza.
- **Audi Quattro Gr.4:** no front anti-roll bar setting and no brake cylinder settings any more;
  different LSD ramp options.
- **Default setups changed on every car.** Use the bundled defaults, not a remembered 0.6 one.
  The Impreza's and the Fulvia's default final drive changed too.

## Carried over from 0.6, not yet confirmed on 0.7

The tyre rules below were found by driving 0.6. Nothing in the 0.7 files contradicts them, but
they have not been re-checked in 0.7. Use them, and when a driver's gauge readings disagree,
trust the readings (*Finding the right cold pressure for a stage*).

- Tyres have a thermal model and a wear model. A tyre heats up over the run, and its pressure
  rises with its temperature. The pressure you set is the cold pressure; the pressure the tyre
  runs at is higher.
- Soft tarmac tyres wear out before the end of a long stage.

## Tyre pressure

- **Tarmac:** the optimal hot pressure is **28 psi, front and rear**. Set the cold pressure
  below it so the tyre reaches 28 during the run: start from **27 psi**, both axles, on every
  tarmac stage. That cold value is a starting guess; the gauge routine below settles it. Make
  the value legal on the car's catalog grid; if 27 is not on the grid, take the nearest legal
  value below it.
- **Gravel, snow:** no version rule. Keep the bundled default's pressures unless a symptom
  points at them.
- This replaces the base principles' "hold the default's pressures" advice on tarmac. The
  bundled default's tarmac pressures were tuned for the old model and are not the target.

## Tyre type on tarmac

- **Stage under 8 km:** `Tarmac Soft`.
- **Stage of 8 km or more:** `Tarmac Medium`. Soft does not last the whole stage.
- The stage length is on the stage's page in the catalogue. When no stage was given, or the
  page has no length, ask in one short line ("about how long is the stage?") and use
  `Tarmac Soft` if the user doesn't know.
- **Medium tyres, on any surface, take longer to warm up.** Tell the driver, before the run, that
  the first kilometres are on cold tyres and to be careful until the grip comes in.
- Wet, winter and snow compounds are chosen as before, from the conditions.

## Finding the right cold pressure for a stage

The best way to set a stage's pressure is a few test runs with the in-game tyre pressure gauge:

1. Run the stage on the cold pressure from *Tyre pressure* above.
2. Near the end of the run, read the gauge for each tyre.
3. Move the cold pressure by the difference: if the gauge shows 29, lower the cold pressure by
   1 psi; if it shows 27, raise it by 1. Front and rear separately if they differ.
4. Run again until the end-of-run reading sits on 28.

A driver who reports a hot reading is reporting a pressure symptom: move the cold pressure by the
difference before anything on the fix-order ladder.
